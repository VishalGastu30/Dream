It didn't start with neural networks or reinforcement learning. It started with a simple fascination for how things are built under the hood. Growing up in Chennai, I spent hours trying to understand how software systems logically piece together to create seamless experiences.

The real turning point wasn't just learning to code, it was realizing that code could be used to solve complex, systemic problems. From the very beginning, I was drawn to the backend — the hidden infrastructure that makes everything else possible.

That early curiosity evolved into a deep interest in software engineering and artificial intelligence. I wanted to move beyond just writing scripts and start architecting systems that could learn, adapt, and scale.
