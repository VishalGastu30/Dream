The turning point came when I realized I didn't just want to build apps; I wanted to build intelligence. I started diving deep into Large Language Models, but I was frustrated by their unpredictable nature. That frustration led to CRAFT.

By combining reinforcement learning with deterministic execution verifiers, I found a way to eliminate reward noise in small language models. I managed to push the accuracy of a 3.8B model significantly higher and deployed it as a lightweight CPU artifact. It was a breakthrough moment—proving that you don't always need massive compute to achieve meaningful AI capabilities.

Simultaneously, I built Synergy and Viren, proving that I could handle both large-scale full-stack systems and secure, on-device mobile applications. The ability to span the entire stack, from a React frontend down to on-device LLM inference, completely changed how I approach problem-solving.
