My formal journey took shape at the SRM Institute of Science and Technology. Pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science gave me the rigorous foundation I needed — data structures, operating systems, and database management.

But the classroom was only half the story. I threw myself into hackathons like Hack The Knight and Smart India Hackathon. These high-pressure environments forced me to learn fast, fail quickly, and build robust full-stack applications on tight deadlines.

During my time at Qube Cinema Technologies, I experienced the reality of enterprise-grade software. Building Go-based REST APIs and optimizing complex PostgreSQL queries taught me what it takes to maintain reliable, performant systems in production. It was no longer just about making it work; it was about making it scalable.
